I am excited to share that on **16 October 2026** I am starting as a **Postdoctoral Research Assistant (PDRA) in AI Security** at **[Queen Mary University of London (QMUL)](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/)**, in the **[School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/eecs/)**.

I am joining the **[SAYED Systems Group](https://sayed-sys-lab.github.io/)** (Scalable Adaptive Yet Efficient Distributed Systems) under the supervision of **[Prof. Ahmed M. A. Sayed](https://ahmedcs.github.io/)** ([QMUL profile](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/eecs/people/profiles/sayedahmed.html)), where I will work on **decentralised cyber security for distributed systems**. The group works on systems for ML, federated and distributed learning, edge AI, and the security of AI systems; you can see more of its work on the [group projects page](https://sayed-sys-lab.github.io/projects/).

I am looking forward to this next chapter in AI security research in London.
