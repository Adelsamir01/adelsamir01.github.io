I am pleased to share that my paper, **[“APIOT: Autonomous Vulnerability Management Across Bare-Metal Industrial OT Networks”](/assets/pdf/apiot.pdf)**, has been submitted to ***[Computers & Security](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-security)***.

The paper presents **APIOT**, an LLM-agent framework for autonomous discovery, exploitation, network-level mitigation, and verification on bare-metal industrial OT devices. This submission builds on our earlier [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02346).

Co-authors: Budi Arief, Shujun Li, George Oikonomou, and James Pope.
