On **23 September 2026**, I gave a special talk at the **[University Innovation Summit](https://uisummit.gie.xyz)** (UIS 2026), hosted at **[Zewail City of Science and Technology](https://www.zewailcity.edu.eg/)** in 6th of October City, Egypt.

The summit brought together **2,000+ participants** and **100+ youth-led, research-backed projects** across national technology clusters supported by the [Academy of Scientific Research and Technology (ASRT)](https://ror.org/02k284p70), with exhibitions, judging, panels, and an awards ceremony shaping Egypt’s innovation economy.

My session — **“Research in the Age of Capable AI”** — was part of the afternoon **Special Talks** slot on the [summit agenda](https://uisummit.gie.xyz). I walked through how capable AI is reshaping research writing, peer review, funding, and PhD practice, and closed with a practical walk-away toolkit: own the question, verify everything, use AI as a critic not a ghostwriter, disclose and document, protect your ideas and data, and build proof beyond papers.
